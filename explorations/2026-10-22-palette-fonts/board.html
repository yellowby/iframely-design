Data API

Machine-ready URL data processing: declared, signed and logged

Iframely works with publishers, not around them.

Declare why you're reading a URL. We fetch it with a signed, named agent, check robots.txt, Content Signals, the licence and Publisher Console settings, and return what fits both your purpose and the publisher's signals, in five fixed content types: metadata, media links, entities, an excerpt or Markdown full text.

No brute force. The access object shows which publisher signals were found and how each was applied: an audit trail for your legal team, and a debug view for publishers.
